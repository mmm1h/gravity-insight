## Outcome

Every item in the approved CT01 inventory has an independently authored Gravity Skill specification or an explicit safe alternative, with machine-readable dependency, policy, provenance and readiness state.

## Current Baseline

CT02 proves the content schema and dependency model on representative shapes. The remaining inventory may include topics outside Runtime scope, missing Gravity capabilities, unavailable Context or legally blocked source use.

## Scope

- Map every inventory item to a unique Gravity Skill ID/taxonomy or explicit safe alternative.
- Independently specify problem, method structure, dependencies, stop conditions, claims and output.
- Record exact blockers and next evidence for non-executable items.
- Group executable validation into waves by dependency shape.
- Maintain source diff impact without rewriting stable prior versions silently.

## Non-goals

- No requirement that all specified Skills become executable or validated immediately.
- No lowering of Trust, DQ, completeness, privacy, license or claim gates to satisfy coverage.
- No dynamic Runtime schema generated from vendor categories.

## Machine Contract

Coverage is derived from CT01 snapshot identities. Minimum state is `specification=specified`; lifecycle/readiness/validation remain independent. Removed sources become provenance/history events, not automatic Skill deletion. Source changes require reviewed version impact.

## Safety And Operations

Only approved independently authored content is distributable. Vendor examples and marketing claims never enter default output or evidence. High-risk action, model, instrumentation and engineering topics remain blocked or proposal-only until their dependencies exist.

## Acceptance

- Inventory-to-Skill/alternative mapping has zero unexplained items.
- Every specification has complete dependency and claim contracts.
- Blockers are stable, safe and machine-readable.
- No protected prose or unsupported effect claims are present.
- Executable/validated totals are derived, not targets used to bypass gates.

## Verification

Coverage closure, duplicate/unmapped IDs, schema validation, provenance/license, marketing leakage, dependency resolution, claim policy and sampled Journey/Eval gates.

## Rollback And Exit

Content waves can stop at precise blockers without changing platform status. Superseded specifications point to replacements and preserve historical package digests/Receipts.

## Canonical Owners

Full Skill source tree, Hub catalog/packages, migration matrix, readiness reports and content governance documentation.
